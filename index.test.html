Send to Companion app

  
DeckWriter can't reach Companion directly, and Companion only imports files. This small Mac app closes the gap. Click it after you save a page and it:

  
  1. Renames every DeckWriter export in Downloads to .companionconfig
  2. Opens Companion's Import / Export page
  3. Shows the newest file in Finder, ready to pick or drag in

  
Companion address

  Install: paste this into Terminal
  

  
It installs to Applications in your home folder and opens there so you can drag it to your Dock. The first time you click it, macOS asks to let it use your Downloads folder. To remove it, delete the app.
